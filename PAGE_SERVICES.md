## 3. Collaborative Workflow (The 4-Step Process)

A dedicated breakdown section showing how BlackLens works with clients:
1. **01 / Briefing & Concept:** Understanding the core message, audience, and visual references.
2. **02 / Pre-Production:** Shot lists, location scouting, crew & gear planning.
3. **03 / Production Day:** Efficient, focused shooting on set or on location.
4. **04 / Post-Production & Delivery:** Rough cut review, fine tuning, color grade, and final delivery.
